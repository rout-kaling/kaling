A small daily habit

Reading plans that read like stories

Guided plans across the Gita, Ramayana and Mahabharata - from a 52-day whole-Gita path to short plans for a phase of life. Each day is one complete reading: the story first, the meaning after.

A verse for today

Start from the Home screen with a verse for the day, then continue your reading when you have a moment.

Festivals and sacred days

A living calendar of days like Ekadashi and Mahalaya, so you always know what is coming up.

Temples across India

Browse thousands of temples across India, with details and photos.

Reflections that stay private

After each reading, write what stayed with you. Your diary and progress live on your phone, not on our servers.
